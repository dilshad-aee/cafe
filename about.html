About Malabar Bakes

A journey of passion, flavor, and community — serving Pannicode's favorite treats for over 12 years.

Who We Are

Malabar Bakes is a neighborhood bakery and café at Pannikkode Bus Stop, Kozhikode. We're known for our fresh shawarma, crispy fried chicken, custom cakes, natural juices, and everyday baked goods — all prepared fresh daily.

Over the years, we've built a loyal community of food lovers who trust us for quality, taste, and consistency. Every item on our menu is made with carefully selected ingredients and a whole lot of care.

Whether you're picking up your morning snack, ordering cakes for a celebration, or craving a late-night shawarma — we're here for you, 8AM to 10PM, every single day.
